# Real Estate Platform — Product & Admin Workspaces

**Product area:** Real-estate digital product and administration  
**Private workspaces:** [Fragmented Real Estate](https://github.com/Chan0304/fragmented-real-estate-kods) · [One Property Admin](https://github.com/Chan0304/one-property-admin)

## Product family context
Fragmented Real Estate is documented as a real-estate platform prototype. One Property Admin is documented as an administrative interface for a property-oriented product. They are grouped as a related product and administration family; their exact integration and release relationship remains to be confirmed.

## Product problem and intended value
Real-estate workflows can span public discovery and internal property operations. A coherent product family should connect user-facing journeys with reliable administrative processes while keeping data and permissions consistent.

## Scope to validate
Property and listing information, enquiry or lead handling, internal administration, user and role management, status updates, and reporting are candidate areas. The exact supported entities and workflows must be confirmed from the source repositories.

## Product-management approach
Define the target customer and internal user groups, map discovery and administration journeys, agree on the property data model, define ownership and permissions, prioritise the core workflow, and validate end-to-end consistency between user-facing and admin experiences.

## Key controls
Protect owner, tenant, buyer, and lead information; maintain data accuracy and change history; define role-based access; and ensure that any external listing or communication integrations are authorised.

## Status and evidence
The repository documentation confirms a real-estate prototype and a property administration workspace. It does not independently verify a shared platform, implemented listing or transaction features, production deployment, client acceptance, or business outcomes.
