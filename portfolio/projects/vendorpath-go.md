# VendorPath Go — Vendor Management Experience

**Product area:** Vendor management and workflow experience  
**Private source workspace:** [vendorpath-go](https://github.com/Chan0304/vendorpath-go)

## Product context
VendorPath Go is a vendor-oriented product workspace. Its repository documentation identifies vendor management as the product area, while the exact workflows and integrations remain to be validated.

## Product problem and intended value
Businesses that work with external vendors need consistent onboarding, information management, communication, and operational follow-through. A vendor platform should make responsibilities and status visible while protecting business information.

## Scope to validate
Vendor onboarding and profiles, document and compliance records, catalogue or service information, order or work tracking, communication, performance visibility, and administrative controls are candidate scope areas. Do not treat these as implemented until confirmed in the source.

## Product-management approach
Identify vendor and internal-user personas, map onboarding and ongoing lifecycle journeys, define required data and validation rules, establish permissions, prioritise core workflows, and validate exceptions and audit history.

## Key controls
Protect vendor and commercial data, apply least-privilege access, maintain traceable status changes, and define ownership for records and approvals.

## Status and evidence
The repository README describes a vendor management and workflow experience. Feature completeness, integrations, production deployment, client acceptance, and measured outcomes are not independently verified.
