## Product family context
These repositories share a solar-sector product theme and are grouped into one portfolio family to avoid counting related applications and iterations as separate launches. Their exact product boundaries, ownership, and release relationships require validation.

## Product areas indicated by repository documentation
- Solar operations workspace.
- Solar business digital experience.
- Solar insights experience.
- Solar-branded application experience and companion build.

Analytics, energy-generation or consumption data, customer dashboards, monitoring, and live integrations must be confirmed from implementation before being described as supported features.

## Product-management approach
Clarify the product family architecture and canonical repository, identify user groups and business outcomes, map the customer and operations journeys, define data sources and integration contracts, prioritise shared capabilities, and establish a release and support model.

## Key considerations
Data freshness and accuracy, clear ownership of operational records, permissions, customer privacy, resilience of integrations, and understandable presentation of any energy or performance metrics are important product requirements.

## Status and evidence
The repository documentation confirms a set of related solar-sector workspaces but does not establish that they form one deployed platform. Implementation, integrations, deployment, client acceptance, and business outcomes are not independently verified. The generated-suffix repository is treated as a companion build rather than a separate product.
