# TestLadr — Assessment and Exam-Management Platform

**Product area:** Digital assessments, question-paper workflows, and administration

## Product context
TestLadr is the product name used for an assessment and exam-management platform previously referred to as iScorre. The project work includes exam-oriented workflows and administrative capabilities.

## Product areas documented
- Paper and question content upload for competitive-exam and class-based sections.
- Public and draft content states.
- Administrative and sub-administrator access.
- Gamification demonstration workflows.
- Platform review and release-readiness coordination.

These areas are recorded as project scope and work items; the exact live implementation and availability of each capability must be validated before making feature-level claims.

## Product-management considerations
- Define question and paper metadata, validation rules, and publishing permissions.
- Separate draft authoring from public release.
- Establish role-based access for administrators and sub-administrators.
- Validate assessment integrity, content accuracy, and user-facing error states.
- Test the complete authoring-to-publishing journey and record release decisions.

## Delivery and handover
Maintain traceability from requirements to acceptance criteria, test cases, defects, release notes, and operational ownership. App-store review feedback and platform-specific release issues should be tracked as explicit release blockers until resolved.
