# Royale Hayat — Healthcare Digital Experience

**Product area:** Healthcare website and administration experience

## Product context
A digital experience workspace for a healthcare organisation, covering patient-facing information architecture and related administrative workflows.

## Product considerations
Healthcare experiences require clear navigation, accessible content, accurate service information, and careful handling of personal or health-related information. Administrative workflows must use appropriate role permissions and data controls.

## Delivery framework
- Discovery: align stakeholder goals, user groups, service priorities, and constraints.
- Product definition: document scope, user journeys, roles, requirements, and acceptance criteria.
- UX and technical design: validate information architecture, responsive layouts, data flows, and integration boundaries.
- Delivery: implement in reviewable increments and maintain a prioritised backlog.
- Validation: test critical journeys, accessibility, performance, security, and content accuracy.
- Release and handover: confirm deployment readiness, operational ownership, support procedures, and known limitations.

## Key risks and controls
- Protect patient and staff information; do not place personal or health data in public artefacts.
- Validate all clinical and service content with authorised stakeholders.
- Confirm the status of any third-party integrations before representing them as operational.
- Maintain an auditable review and approval process for content changes.
