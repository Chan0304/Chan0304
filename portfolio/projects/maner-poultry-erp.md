# Maner Poultry ERP

**Product area:** Poultry operations and business process management  
**Private source workspace:** [maner-ventures-poultry](https://github.com/Chan0304/maner-ventures-poultry)

## Product context
Maner Poultry ERP is a business application workspace intended to support the management of poultry operations and related business processes.

## Business problem
Poultry businesses coordinate operational activities across multiple functions. A well-defined ERP needs consistent records, process ownership, timely operational visibility, and traceable transactions rather than disconnected manual records.

## Product scope to validate
Potential functional areas for discovery and requirements definition include flock and batch records, feed and consumables inventory, procurement and supplier records, production tracking, sales and dispatch, expenses and reporting, user roles, access control, and audit history. These are candidate ERP domains, not a statement that each module is currently implemented.

## Product-management approach
1. Map the current operating process and system boundaries.
2. Define user roles across operations, inventory, procurement, sales, and finance.
3. Document master data, transaction lifecycles, validations, and exception flows.
4. Prioritise workflows by operational impact and dependency.
5. Define acceptance criteria and reconciliation rules for inventory and financial records.
6. Validate end-to-end flows with representative operational scenarios.
7. Prepare training, support, and handover materials.

## Key controls and risks
Inventory accuracy, traceability of flock/batch records, role-based permissions, auditability of stock adjustments, financial reconciliation, and reliable backups should be treated as core non-functional requirements. Sensitive supplier, employee, and business data must remain private.
