# Secret Hair Care — Personalised Hair-Care Experience

**Product area:** Consumer digital experience and personalised hair-care journey  
**Private source workspaces:** [Secret Hair Journey](https://github.com/Chan0304/secret-hair-journey) · [Secret Hair Whisper](https://github.com/Chan0304/secret-hair-whisper)

## Product context
Secret Hair Care is a product-family workspace focused on a personalised hair-care experience. The two private repositories are treated as related workspaces or iterations until their exact relationship and release boundaries are confirmed.

## Product problem and intended value
People often need help organising hair-care information and maintaining a consistent routine. A digital experience in this domain should make the user journey understandable, support relevant guidance, and communicate the limits of recommendations clearly.

## Product scope to validate
Possible areas for product discovery and requirements definition include user onboarding and hair-care goals, profile and preference capture, routine planning and progress tracking, educational content and product information, personalised recommendations where supported by validated inputs and rules, reminders and feedback, and administration/content management. These are candidate capabilities, not claims that all features are implemented or medically validated.

## Product-management considerations
- Make clear whether content is educational, cosmetic, or professional advice.
- Avoid medical diagnosis or unsupported treatment claims.
- Explain what user information is collected and why.
- Obtain appropriate consent and provide clear privacy and deletion controls.
- Make recommendation logic understandable and avoid implying guaranteed results.
- Ensure accessibility, inclusive content, and responsive mobile journeys.

## Delivery approach
Define target users and key use cases, map onboarding and recurring journeys, prioritise the minimum viable experience, document recommendation inputs and limitations, validate usability and privacy, and test the complete user journey before release.

## Repository relationship
The secret-hair-journey and secret-hair-whisper repositories are both associated with the Secret Hair Care product family. Until their individual scope is confirmed, they are represented as one case study to avoid double-counting related iterations.

## Status and evidence
The repository documentation does not independently verify feature completeness, recommendation accuracy, production release, user adoption, or measured outcomes. Screenshots, user data, and recommendation examples should be reviewed and redacted before public disclosure.

## Confidentiality
Source code, user information, internal product artefacts, and configuration remain in private workspaces.
