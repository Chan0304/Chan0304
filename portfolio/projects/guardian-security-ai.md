# Guardian — Security & AI Product Family

**Product area:** Security monitoring and AI-assisted security workspace  
**Private workspaces:** [Guardian Sentinel](https://github.com/Chan0304/guardian-sentinel) · [Guardian Aegis AI](https://github.com/Chan0304/guardian-aegis-ai)

## Product family context
Guardian Sentinel is documented as a security and monitoring application. Guardian Aegis AI is documented as an AI-oriented companion or iteration in the Guardian product family. They are grouped here as related workspaces, not represented as two separate production products.

## Product direction
The documented themes suggest a security-focused experience with operational visibility and a related AI-assisted intelligence workspace. Exact user journeys, data sources, AI provider, and integrations must be verified before defining detailed capabilities.

## Discovery and scope priorities
Identify target users and security use cases, define monitored entities and event sources, establish alert and escalation workflows, clarify the AI assistant's role and limitations, and define the human review process.

## Safety and security requirements
Access to security information must be tightly controlled. AI-generated summaries or suggestions should be traceable to source data and must not replace authorised security decisions. Define data retention, audit logging, false-positive handling, incident escalation, and secure integration practices.

## Status and evidence
The two repository descriptions indicate a related product family, but do not establish a shared deployment or completed integration. Feature completeness, production readiness, customer acceptance, and measurable outcomes require verification.
