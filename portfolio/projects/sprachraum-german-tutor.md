# Sprachraum — German Language Learning

**Product area:** Language learning and tutoring  
**Private source workspace:** [sprachraum-german-tutor](https://github.com/Chan0304/sprachraum-german-tutor)

## Product context
Sprachraum is a German-language tutoring product workspace intended to help learners practise German in a structured way and access learning support.

## Product objectives
- Provide an accessible learning experience for German-language learners.
- Organise learning content by proficiency level and learning objective.
- Support repeatable practice and learner progress visibility.
- Provide a foundation for tutor or administrator content management.

## Intended users
Learners, tutors, and platform administrators. Supported roles and permissions require confirmation from the application.

## Candidate learning capabilities
Level-based learning paths, vocabulary and grammar practice, listening and reading exercises, speaking activities, assessments, learner progress, tutor feedback, and learning-content administration are candidate scope areas. Validate each against the codebase before describing it as available.

## Product quality considerations
Content should be accurate, level-appropriate, accessible, and reviewed by qualified language educators. Assessment criteria and learner progress indicators should be transparent. Personal learner information should be protected through appropriate access and retention controls.

## Delivery approach
Define learner personas and proficiency levels, map the learning journey, prioritise core practice loops, validate content and assessment design, conduct usability testing with learners, and establish content ownership and operational support.

## Status and evidence
The repository describes a German tutoring experience. Specific lesson, assessment, tutor, or AI capabilities, production deployment, adoption, and learning outcomes are not independently verified.
