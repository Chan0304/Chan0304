# TechSchool — Technology Learning Platform

**Product area:** Technical education and practical learning

## Product direction
TechSchool is a technology learning initiative intended to organise technical education into structured learning paths, practical exercises, and guided lab experiences.

## Intended user groups
- Learners who need structured courses and hands-on practice.
- Instructors or mentors who manage learning content and support learners.
- Administrators who manage catalogues, cohorts, enrolments, and platform operations.
- Education partners who need visibility into programme participation and progress.

## Candidate product modules
Course catalogue, learning paths, learner dashboard, lesson delivery, quizzes and assessments, practical labs, progress tracking, instructor tools, administration, notifications, and reporting are candidate scope areas. They should not be represented as delivered until verified in the application.

## Product lifecycle
1. Validate learner personas, programme outcomes, and course taxonomy.
2. Define lesson formats, assessment rules, and completion criteria.
3. Map learner, instructor, and administrator journeys.
4. Deliver course and progress capabilities incrementally.
5. Validate content quality, accessibility, privacy, and assessment integrity.
6. Pilot with a defined learner cohort and incorporate feedback.
7. Prepare launch, support, and content-maintenance ownership.

## Success measures to define
Potential measures include learner activation, lesson completion, assessment participation, course completion, learner satisfaction, and repeat engagement. No numerical outcome is claimed here.
