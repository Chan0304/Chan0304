# GoScore — Study-Abroad Digital Platform

**Product area:** Education services and student enquiry journey  
**Public reference:** [GoScore website](https://goscore.study/)

## Product context
GoScore is a digital platform workspace focused on helping prospective students explore overseas education information and connect with guidance services.

## User groups
Prospective students, parents or guardians, counsellors, education partners, and platform administrators. Final role permissions should be confirmed against the approved product specification.

## Product problem and goals
The product aims to make study-abroad information easier to discover, create a clear path from exploration to enquiry, and support timely follow-up by the relevant team.

## Journey to design and validate
1. Discover destination, institution, and programme information.
2. Compare relevant options and understand next steps.
3. Submit an enquiry or request counselling.
4. Route the enquiry to an authorised team member.
5. Track follow-up and communicate next actions to the student.

## Product-management considerations
- Keep admissions and visa information dated and traceable to authoritative sources.
- Distinguish general information from personalised advice and avoid guaranteeing outcomes.
- Define consent and communication preferences at enquiry capture.
- Protect student contact details and documents through role-based access and retention controls.
- Validate mobile usability, accessibility, form reliability, and content governance.

## Delivery approach
Align stakeholders on target regions and student segments, approve the content model, define the enquiry workflow and ownership, validate roles and integrations, test the end-to-end journey, and prepare operational content and support handover.

## Evidence and status
The public website is a project reference, not proof that every candidate module or integration described here is live. Specific feature claims, adoption figures, conversion metrics, and delivery ownership require supporting evidence before publication.
